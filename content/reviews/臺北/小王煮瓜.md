There's a billion 滷肉飯 places all over Taiwan, and everyone has their own favorite gem. 小王 is definitely a touristy place, but I truthfully have not eaten a lot of 滷肉飯 in my life, so naturally I wanted to try the most popular place first. The main motivation for me to go was from Eric Sze on his instagram, and I must say this place was great. I was only going to get a lu rou fan, but seeing the black gold braising liquid I couldn't resist getting braised bamboo shoots and oil tofu as well. Everything was deeply savory with caramelized notes, and the lu rou was satisfyingly gelatinous, making my mouth sticky with every bite.
